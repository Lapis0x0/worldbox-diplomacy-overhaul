A 2x2 sprite sheet of 4 game UI icons for the pixel-art god-simulation game WorldBox, drawn exactly in the style of the two reference images, which show original WorldBox icons (each a 28x28 pixel-art sprite scaled up 16x). Match them precisely: chunky square pixels on a strict 28x28 grid, each art pixel a crisp square block, limited palette, bold readable silhouette, dark outline on the shadow side, simple 2-3 tone shading, light from the upper left, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text.

The icons are government types shown as kingdom traits. They form one family, REPUBLICS, so they share one main element: a small classical assembly hall seen from the front, a triangular pediment roof on top of four thick columns standing on a stepped base, built of warm cream-white stone with light brown shading (like the stone and wood tones in the references). Draw this hall identically in icons 1-3; only the addition changes.

1. Republic: the assembly hall alone, with a small red banner on a short pole on the roof peak.
2. Merchant republic: the same hall, with one big shiny gold coin (like the reference gold coin) leaning in front of its lower right corner, overlapping the steps.
3. Military republic: the same hall, with two crossed swords (like the reference crossed swords) in front of its lower part, overlapping the columns.
4. An alternative design of the plain republic hall for comparison: a rounder dome-roofed senate building with columns, same colors.

Each icon centered in its own equal square cell, compact, the hall filling about 55% of the cell width, nothing touching the cell borders. Background flat uniform medium grey (#808080), no shadows on the background, no borders, no labels.
