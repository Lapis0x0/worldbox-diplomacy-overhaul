# FAQ

**Why has an empire started to decline?**

Every dynasty builds up Decay, and aging speeds up late in its life, so even the best dynasty declines eventually. Losing wars, losing cities, a weak ruler, and falling vassal Integration all speed up the decline. Win more wars, or wait for the ruler to start a Restoration. See [Rise and Fall of Dynasties](cycle.md).

**Why are cities rebelling one after another?**

In Decline, the lower the Mandate, the more city loyalty is lost, and the cities with the lowest loyalty rebel first. Cities of a clan also become unhappy when parliament passes policies that are far from the clan's stance ("Opposed policies") or moves troops and money to the center ("Curbed privileges"). A city's loyalty breakdown shows the reasons. See [Parliament and Policies](parliament.md#local-discontent).

**Why did a vassal become independent?**

A vassal starts an Independence Plot when its opinion of the overlord is low and the overlord cannot hold it down. An overlord in Decline, a vassal with deep resentment, an overlord fighting elsewhere, and a Vassal Tribute set too high all contribute. Check the vassal's opinion breakdown and its Integration trait. See [Vassals](vassals.md#independence).

**Why did a kingdom grant a city it had just taken?**

When a kingdom has more cities than vanilla's governing limit, most newly taken cities are granted as vassals instead of being annexed. Passing Direct Rule in parliament reduces grants. See [Vassals](vassals.md#where-vassals-come-from).

**Why don't my armies attack?**

When the Army policy is not Noble Retinues, local armies mainly defend the border and attack only when their strength is at least that of the enemy city's garrison; campaigns are carried out mainly by the Guard. Cities and kingdoms with unpaid troops also do not recruit. You can order an army to attack directly; see [Commanding Armies](commands.md).

**Why are my armies shrinking?**

Troops may be unpaid. When the treasury or a city stockpile cannot pay, some soldiers are discharged each year, and the rest deal less damage. The kingdom and city windows show unpaid troops. See [Treasury, Pay and Public Works](treasury.md).

**Why does a war not end?**

Peace Talks can begin only after 3 years of war. The side ahead refuses if the war score has not reached what it expects. Warlike kings expect more, but the longer the war and the heavier the losses, the lower the expectation falls. Total War has no peace talks. See [War and Peace Talks](war.md).

**Why can't a large city be taken?**

In vanilla, as long as one defender remains in the city, siege progress is stuck at 5%. Now the attacking captain goes after the defenders, and when the attackers outnumber the defenders 5 to 1 the city falls outright. See [War and Peace Talks](war.md#sieges).

**Why did a republic become an empire?**

A republic at its Prime may see an ambitious consul crown himself or herself emperor. This can be turned off by turning off "Historic upheavals" in the settings. See [Governments and Rulers](government.md#republic-crowns-an-emperor).

**Why did my attack order disappear after loading a save?**

Attack orders are not saved. After loading, armies return to their previous behavior. Garrison and camp orders are saved.

**Do old saves work?**

Yes. Saves made before installing the mod can be loaded as they are, and missing data is filled in automatically in game: the government is decided by culture, large kingdoms count as Prime, and existing vassals start as Estranged.
