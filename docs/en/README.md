# Diplomacy Overhaul Player Guide

Diplomacy Overhaul gives WorldBox kingdoms internal politics and foreign relations. Large kingdoms grant vassals and demand tribute from weak neighbors. Kingdoms have governments and parliaments, and clans vote on policy. Dynasties rise and decline, and finally fall apart in a Great Rebellion. Wars end in negotiated treaties once they have run long enough. A treasury pays for the central Guard. You can just watch, step in with god powers, or command armies directly.

## What you will see after installing

- **Kingdom names include the government.** For example "Duchy of Coimbra" or "Empire of Castile". The kingdom window subtitle shows the government. A vassal adds its overlord's name, and a large kingdom adds its dynasty phase, such as "Empire · Prime".
- **New kingdom traits.** Government, Dynastic Cycle (Renewal, Prime, Decline, Collapse), and vassal Integration (Resentful, Estranged, Settled, United). Hover over a trait to see the exact numbers.
- **New tabs on the left of the kingdom window.** Parliament, and Elections & Offices; the treasury page sits above the Interesting People tab.
- **New items in opinion and loyalty breakdowns.** "Loyal to overlord", "Overlord's fortunes", "Opposed policies", "Curbed privileges", and more.
- **New world log events.** Submission, grants of land, restoration, Great Rebellion, change of government, crowning an emperor, treaties.
- **A "Diplomacy Overhaul" tab at the bottom.** The god powers and settings are in it.

Saves made before installing the mod can be used as they are. Missing data is filled in automatically in game.

## How the systems connect

- **The dynastic cycle** affects vassal Integration, city loyalty, and the chance of rebellion. A republic at its Prime may crown an emperor, and in Collapse a general may seize power.
- **Vassals** come from surrender, grants of land, and submission. An overlord and its vassals enter and leave wars together. When the dynasty declines, vassals drift away, and in a Great Rebellion they break away in numbers.
- **Wars** end by negotiation. Depending on the war score, the loser pays reparations, cedes vassals, or submits.
- **Parliament** decides taxation, army organization, land grants, and foreign policy. Clans can form blocs that vote together.
- **The treasury** receives the money city lords send up, and pays for the Guard, plots, and reparations.
