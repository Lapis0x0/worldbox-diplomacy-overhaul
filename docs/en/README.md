# Diplomacy Overhaul Player Guide

Diplomacy Overhaul gives WorldBox kingdoms internal politics and foreign relations. Large kingdoms grant vassals and pressure weak neighbors into submission. Vassals can grow loyal or rebel. Kingdoms have governments and parliaments, and clans argue over policy. Dynasties rise, decline, and finally fall apart in a Great Rebellion. Wars end in negotiated treaties once they have run long enough. Money flows into a treasury, and the treasury pays for the central Guard. You can just watch, step in with god powers, or command armies directly.

## What you will see after installing

- **Kingdom names include the government.** For example "Duchy of Coimbra" or "Empire of Castile". The kingdom window subtitle shows the government. A vassal adds its overlord's name. A large kingdom adds its dynasty phase, such as "Empire · Prime".
- **New kingdom traits.** Government, Dynastic Cycle (Renewal, Prime, Decline, Collapse), and vassal Integration (Resentful, Estranged, Settled, United). Hover over a trait to see the exact numbers.
- **Two new tabs on the left of the kingdom window.** Parliament, and Elections & Offices.
- **A Treasury line in the kingdom window.** It sits under the leader's wealth.
- **New items in opinion and loyalty breakdowns.** "Loyal to overlord", "Overlord's fortunes", "Opposed policies", "Curbed privileges" and more. All of them appear in the vanilla breakdown windows.
- **Many new world log events.** Submission, grants of land, restoration, Great Rebellion, change of government, crowning an emperor, treaties.
- **A "Diplomacy Overhaul" tab at the bottom.** The god powers and settings are in it.

Saves made before installing the mod work as they are. Missing data is filled in automatically in game.

## How the systems connect

- **The dynastic cycle is the center.** In a kingdom at its Prime, vassals are loyal and cities are calm. In a declining kingdom, vassals drift away and cities rebel, until a Great Rebellion breaks out. A republic at its Prime may crown an emperor. In Collapse, a general may seize power.
- **Vassals are the basic unit of diplomacy.** Surrender, grants of land, and submission all create vassals. An overlord and its vassals enter and leave wars together. When the dynasty declines, vassals drift away. In a Great Rebellion they break away in numbers.
- **Wars end by negotiation.** When a war is nearly decided, the two sides talk. Depending on the war score, the loser pays reparations, cedes vassals, or submits.
- **Clans decide policy.** Clans get seats in proportion to the cities they hold. They vote on taxation, army organization, land grants, and foreign policy. As a kingdom expands, local clans can grow strong enough to rival the royal house.
- **Money has somewhere to go.** City lords send money to the treasury. The treasury pays for the Guard, starts plots, and pays reparations.
