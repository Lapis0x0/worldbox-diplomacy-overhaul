# Plots

In vanilla, a king considers a plot about once every year or two: all the plots currently possible are put together and one is picked at random. Most plots a vanilla king can start need level 5 or higher, so most of the time nothing happens.

The mod adds several plots and gives every plot a **motive**. Conditions decide whether a plot can be started, and the motive decides how much the king wants to start it. The more urgent the situation and the better the king's personality fits, the more likely he or she is to start it. In the vanilla plot editor you can make a king start a plot regardless of motive.

A city leader's rebellion plot is abandoned once the city is content again. When a monarch dies or a republic's ruler leaves office, loyalty drops across the kingdom; rebellion plots started then mostly fall apart once the new ruler takes over and loyalty recovers. Rebellions forced in the plot editor are not affected.

## New Plots

| Plot | Who starts it | When they want it | Personalities that favor it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independence Plot | A vassal's king | The more discontent with the overlord, the more they want it. More so when the overlord is fighting a big war elsewhere | Warlike, ambitious |
| Demand Tribute | A strong kingdom's king | The stronger it is compared with a neighbor, the more it wants it | Warlike or good at diplomacy, ambitious |
| Peace Talks | The kings of both warring sides | The side ahead wants to talk once the war score is enough. The side behind wants it more the more it is losing and the longer the war lasts | The side behind: good at diplomacy |
| Restoration | The ruler of a declining dynasty | The lower the Mandate, the more they want it | Good at governing |
| Royal Appanage | A monarchy's king | The more cities beyond the governing limit, the more they want it | Good at governing |
| Direct Rule | An overlord with provinces | The further from the governing limit, the more they want it | Good at governing |

- A Content king hardly wants any plot. An Ambitious king wants independence and pressuring others into submission more.
- Parliament policies also affect motive: under the Feudalism Land Grants policy a king wants the Royal Appanage plot more, and under the Direct Rule policy he or she wants the Direct Rule plot more. A vassal whose Fealty is Seek Autonomy wants independence more.
- The money for a plot a king starts comes from the treasury.
- The vanilla "peace negotiation" plot is replaced by Peace Talks.

For the exact conditions and results of each plot, see [Vassals](vassals.md), [Rise and Fall of Dynasties](cycle.md), and [War and Peace Talks](war.md).
