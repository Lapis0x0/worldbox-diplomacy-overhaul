# Plots

A plot is a long-running action started by a king, city lord, or clan member, and its result takes effect when it completes. In vanilla, a king considers a plot about once every year or two, picking at random from the plots currently possible. Most plots a vanilla king can start need level 5 or higher, so most of the time nothing happens.

The mod adds several plots and gives every plot a **motive**. Conditions decide whether a plot can be started, and the motive decides how much the king wants to start it. The more urgent the situation and the better the king's personality fits, the more likely the plot is to be started. In the vanilla plot editor a plot can be forced regardless of motive. Money for plots started by a king comes from the treasury.

A city lord's rebellion plot is abandoned once the city is content again. When a monarch dies or a republic's ruler leaves office, loyalty drops across the kingdom, and rebellion plots started then mostly end once the new ruler takes over and loyalty recovers. Rebellions forced in the plot editor are not affected.

## New Plots

| Plot | Who starts it | Source of motive | Personalities that favor it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independence Plot | A vassal's king | Stronger the more discontent with the overlord. Stronger when the overlord is fighting elsewhere | Warlike, ambitious |
| Demand Tribute | A strong kingdom's king | Stronger the more it outmatches a neighbor | Warlike or good at diplomacy, ambitious |
| Peace Talks | The kings of both warring sides | The side ahead when the war score is sufficient. The side behind, stronger the more it is losing and the longer the war lasts | The side behind: good at diplomacy |
| Restoration | The ruler of a declining dynasty | Stronger the lower the Mandate | Good at governing |
| Royal Appanage | A monarchy's king | Stronger the more cities beyond the governing limit | Good at governing |
| Direct Rule | An overlord with provinces | Stronger the further it is from the governing limit | Good at governing |
| Win Over | A clan chief | Stronger the closer the target is to joining a bloc. Stronger when the clan is small and has no allies | Good at diplomacy, ambitious |
| Leave Bloc | A chief in a bloc, other than the leader | Stronger the more discontent with the bloc | Hotheaded, ambitious |

- A Content king is reluctant to start any plot, and an Ambitious king is more inclined toward independence and demanding tribute.
- Parliament policies also affect motive. Under the Feudalism Land Grants policy a king is more inclined toward Royal Appanage, and under Direct Rule toward the Direct Rule plot. A vassal whose Fealty is Seek Autonomy is more inclined toward independence.
- The vanilla "peace negotiation" plot is replaced by Peace Talks.

For the conditions and results of each plot, see [Vassals](vassals.md), [Rise and Fall of Dynasties](cycle.md), [War and Peace Talks](war.md), and [Blocs](blocs.md).
