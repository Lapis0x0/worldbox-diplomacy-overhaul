# Powers and Settings

All the powers are in the "Diplomacy Overhaul" tab at the bottom, in four groups from left to right: Settings, Vassals, War, and City Management. For powers that take two clicks, an arrow is drawn on the map after the first click, and clicking the first target again cancels the power.

## Vassals

| Power | How to use | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Make Vassal | Click the overlord, then the vassal | The second becomes a vassal of the first |
| Incite Independence | Click a vassal | It immediately starts a war of independence against its overlord |
| Great Rebellion | Click a kingdom | A Great Rebellion breaks out at once |
| Mandate of Heaven | Click a large kingdom | Advances the dynastic cycle one phase; using it again in Decline starts a Great Rebellion |
| Release as Vassal | Click a non-capital city | It becomes a kingdom and a vassal of its former kingdom |

For the rules, see [Vassals](vassals.md) and [Rise and Fall of Dynasties](cycle.md).

## War

| Power | How to use | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Command Army | Click an army, then the target | A foreign city is attacked, a friendly city is garrisoned, open ground becomes a camp, and clicking the post cancels the order |
| Kingdom Offensive | Click the kingdom sending troops, then the target city | All its armies attack a foreign city, or all go to garrison a friendly city |
| Hand Back Command | Click a kingdom | All its armies that follow the player's orders are handed back to the AI |

Without holding a power, armies can also be box-selected and given orders with the right mouse button; see [Commanding Armies](commands.md).

## City Management

| Power | How to use | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer City | Click a city, then the receiving kingdom | The city goes to the receiver; a capital cannot be transferred |
| Annex Kingdom | Click the annexing kingdom, then the kingdom to be annexed | All cities and vassals of the annexed kingdom go to the annexer; if the annexer is itself a vassal, those vassals are freed |

## Settings

Open them with the settings button at the far left of the tab. Each system has one switch, and a change takes effect immediately.

| Section | Switch | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Diplomacy | Grants and submission | Large kingdoms grant cities as vassals, and weak neighbors submit |
| | Peace talks | Long wars end in treaties based on the war score; when off, the vanilla peace negotiation is restored |
| Domestic | Parliament and policies | Clans vote on the kingdom's policies by seats; when off, policies have no effect and tribute follows the vanilla tribute traits |
| | Dynastic cycle | Large kingdoms go through Renewal, Prime, Decline, and Collapse |
| | Government follows power | The government changes with strength and vassal status; governments chosen by hand are kept |
| | Historic upheavals | Extinct royal lines, republics crowning emperors, coups |
| Military | Military overhaul | The Guard, national command, orders and box selection, sieges, armament, and pay; when off, armies behave as in vanilla and the army command powers are removed |
| Display | Government in names | For example, Duchy of Coimbra |
| | Policy notices | A notice at the top left when a kingdom enacts a policy; when off, it is recorded only in world history |
| Performance | Optimize base game | Does not change outcomes. Raises the frame rate on large maps by about 20%, and can be turned off if something behaves strangely |

All switches are on by default, and "Defaults" turns them all back on.
