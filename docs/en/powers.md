# Powers and Settings

All the powers are in the "Diplomacy Overhaul" tab at the bottom. From left to right there are four groups: Settings, Vassals, War, and City Management. For powers that take two clicks, an arrow is drawn on the map after the first target. Click the first target again to cancel.

## Vassals

| Power | How to use | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Make Vassal | Click the overlord, then the vassal | The second becomes a vassal of the first |
| Incite Independence | Click a vassal | It immediately starts a war of independence against its overlord |
| Great Rebellion | Click a kingdom | A Great Rebellion breaks out at once |
| Mandate of Heaven | Click a large kingdom | Advances the dynastic cycle one phase. Using it again in Decline starts a Great Rebellion |
| Release as Vassal | Click a non-capital city | It becomes a kingdom and a vassal of its former kingdom |

See [Vassals](vassals.md) and [Rise and Fall of Dynasties](cycle.md).

## War

| Power | How to use | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Command Army | Click an army, then the target | A foreign city is attacked, a friendly city is garrisoned, open ground becomes a camp, and clicking its post cancels the order |
| Kingdom Offensive | Click the kingdom sending troops, then the target city | All its armies attack a foreign city, or all go to garrison a friendly city |
| Hand Back Command | Click a kingdom | All its armies that follow your orders are handed back to the AI |

You can also box-select armies and right-click to give orders without holding a power, see [Commanding Armies](commands.md).

## City Management

| Power | How to use | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer City | Click a city, then the receiving kingdom | The city goes to the receiver. A capital cannot be transferred |
| Annex Kingdom | Click the annexing kingdom, then the kingdom to be annexed | All cities and vassals of the annexed kingdom go to the annexer. If the annexer is itself a vassal, those vassals are freed |

## Settings

Open them with the settings button at the far left of the tab.

| Group | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Vassals | Vassal opinion bonus toward overlord | 30 |
| | Vassals start to resent their overlord below this opinion | 0 |
| | Overlord counts as declining below this % of vassal power | 120 |
| | Large kingdoms grant cities as vassals and weak neighbours submit | On |
| Peace talks | Warring sides negotiate and sign a treaty based on how the war went | On |
| | Minimum years of war before peace talks | 3 |
| | Reparations take this % of the loser's stockpiles | 20 |
| Government | Update government as the realm grows or becomes a vassal (manual picks are kept) | On |
| | Show government type with kingdom names | On |
| | Governments change with history: extinct royal lines, republics crowning emperors, coups in times of collapse | On |
| Dynastic Cycle | Great realms go through renewal, prime, decline and collapse | On |
| | Restoration plot success chance (%) | 30 |
| Performance | Skip redundant work in the base game for smoother play without changing outcomes | On |

The performance optimizations raise the frame rate on large maps by about 20%, and game results are exactly the same as in vanilla. If you run into strange problems, try turning it off.
